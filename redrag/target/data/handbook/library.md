# Library

The Kestrel Valley College central library is on the ground floor of the Aspen Block.

## Opening hours

The library is open from 8:00 am to 10:00 pm on weekdays and from 10:00 am to 6:00 pm on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays. During the two weeks before end-semester exams, opening hours are extended until midnight.

## Borrowing books

Undergraduate students can borrow up to 4 books at a time for 14 days. Postgraduate students can borrow up to 6 books for 21 days. Books can be renewed twice online unless another student has reserved them. Reference books and journals cannot be taken out of the library.

## Fines

The late fine is Rs 5 per book per day. Lost books must be replaced or paid for at twice the listed price. Students with unpaid library fines cannot collect their hall tickets.

## Other services

The library has 40 desktop computers, a quiet reading room, and four group study rooms that can be booked for up to 2 hours through the library portal.
