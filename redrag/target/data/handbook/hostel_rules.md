# Hostel Rules

Kestrel Valley College has three hostels: Birch House and Cedar House for women, and Maple House for men.

## Timings

Hostel gates close at 10:30 pm every day. Students returning after the gate closes must sign the late-entry register and inform their warden in advance. Three unexplained late entries in a semester lead to a meeting with the chief warden.

## Visitors

Visitors are allowed in the common room only, between 4:00 pm and 7:00 pm. Overnight guests are not permitted. Parents may visit rooms on the first Sunday of every month.

## Room rules

Cooking appliances such as induction stoves and kettles are not allowed in rooms. Each room has a fan, a study table and a cupboard per student. Damage to hostel property is charged to the occupants of the room.

## Leaving the hostel

Students going home for the weekend must fill in the leave form on the hostel portal before Friday 6:00 pm. Room changes are allowed only at the start of a semester.
