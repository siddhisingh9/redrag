# Academic Calendar

## Odd semester

The odd semester begins on the first Monday of August. Mid-semester tests are held in the third week of September and the last week of October. End-semester examinations start on 1 December, and winter break runs from 20 December to 3 January.

## Even semester

The even semester begins on 5 January. Mid-semester tests are held in the second week of February and the last week of March. End-semester examinations start on 2 May, and summer break runs from the last week of May until the end of July.

## Holidays

The college is closed on national holidays, for Diwali week in October or November, and for three days during the annual cultural festival, Kestrel Fest, in February.

## Orientation

First-year orientation is held during the last week of July. Attendance at orientation is compulsory for all new students.
