# IT Services

## Wi-Fi

Campus Wi-Fi is available in all academic blocks, the library and the hostels. Students log in to the network "KVC-Students" with their college email ID and portal password. Each student can connect up to 3 devices.

## College email

Every student gets a college email ID in the format firstname.rollnumber@kvc.example.edu. College email is used for all official announcements, so students should check it daily.

## Password reset

Students who forget their portal password can reset it from the login page using their registered mobile number. If that fails, visit the IT helpdesk in the Admin Block, room 104, between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm on weekdays.

## Computer labs

The computer labs in the Birch Block are open to all students from 9:00 am to 8:00 pm. Installing software on lab computers is not allowed without permission from the lab in-charge.
