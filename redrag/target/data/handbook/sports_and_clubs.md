# Sports and Clubs

## Sports facilities

The sports complex has a cricket ground, a football field, two basketball courts, four badminton courts and a gym. The gym is open from 6:00 am to 9:00 am and from 5:00 pm to 9:00 pm. Students must show their college ID card at the entrance.

## Joining a club

Kestrel Valley College has more than 25 student clubs, including coding, robotics, music, dance, drama, photography and debate. Club recruitment happens in the second week of each semester. Students can join up to three clubs.

## Starting a new club

To start a new club, at least 15 students and one faculty advisor must submit a proposal to the Student Activities Office. New clubs are approved at the start of each semester.

## Kestrel Fest

Kestrel Fest is the annual cultural and technical festival, held over three days in February. Students can volunteer through the Student Activities Office.
