# Scholarships

## Merit scholarship

Students in the top 5 percent of their branch in the previous academic year receive a merit scholarship covering 50 percent of tuition for the next year. No application is needed; the list is published in August.

## Need-based scholarship

Students whose family income is below Rs 6 lakh per year can apply for a need-based scholarship covering up to 75 percent of tuition. Applications open on 1 July and close on 31 July. Income certificates and the last two years of mark sheets are required.

## Sports and cultural scholarship

Students who represent the college at state or national level in sports or cultural events can apply for a fee waiver of up to Rs 25,000 per year.

## Keeping a scholarship

Scholarship holders must keep a CGPA of at least 7.0 and 75 percent attendance. Scholarships are reviewed every year.
