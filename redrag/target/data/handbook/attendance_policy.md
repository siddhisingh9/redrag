# Attendance Policy

## Minimum attendance

Students need at least 75 percent attendance in every course to sit the end-semester examination. Attendance is calculated separately for theory classes and lab sessions.

## Medical leave

Students who miss classes because of illness can apply for medical leave within 5 days of returning, with a certificate from a registered doctor. Approved medical leave can count for up to 10 percent of attendance.

## Condonation

Students with attendance between 65 and 75 percent may apply to the head of department for condonation, with valid reasons and a fee of Rs 2,000. Students below 65 percent must repeat the course.

## Checking attendance

Attendance is updated on the student portal every Friday. Errors must be reported to the course teacher within two weeks.
