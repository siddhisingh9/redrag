# Examination Policy

Each semester has two mid-semester tests and one end-semester examination.

## Weightage

Mid-semester tests together count for 30 percent of the final grade, assignments and lab work count for 20 percent, and the end-semester examination counts for 50 percent.

## Hall tickets

Hall tickets are released on the student portal one week before the end-semester examination. Students need at least 75 percent attendance and no pending fees or library fines to download a hall ticket.

## Exam hall rules

Students must reach the exam hall 15 minutes before the start time and carry their college ID card and hall ticket. Mobile phones, smart watches and notes are not allowed inside the hall. Students arriving more than 30 minutes late are not allowed to enter.

## Re-evaluation and supplementary exams

Students can apply for re-evaluation of an end-semester paper within 7 days of the results, for a fee of Rs 500 per paper. Students who fail a course can take the supplementary examination held in July.
