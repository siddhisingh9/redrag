# Fees FAQ

## When are fees due?

Tuition fees are paid twice a year. The odd-semester fee is due by 1 August and the even-semester fee is due by 15 January.

## How much are the fees?

Tuition is Rs 85,000 per semester for B.Tech programmes and Rs 60,000 per semester for B.Sc programmes. Hostel fees are Rs 40,000 per semester, including mess charges.

## How can I pay?

Fees can be paid online through the fee portal using net banking, UPI or a debit card. Demand drafts are accepted at the accounts office in the Admin Block between 10:00 am and 3:00 pm on weekdays.

## What if I pay late?

A late fee of Rs 100 per day is charged for the first 15 days after the due date. After 30 days, the student's portal access is suspended until the fee is paid.

## Can I get a refund?

Students who withdraw before classes begin get a full refund minus a processing fee of Rs 1,000. No tuition refund is given after the fourth week of the semester.
