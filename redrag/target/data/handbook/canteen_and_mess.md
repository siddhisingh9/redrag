# Canteen and Mess

## Hostel mess

The hostel mess serves breakfast from 7:30 am to 9:30 am, lunch from 12:30 pm to 2:30 pm, snacks from 5:00 pm to 6:00 pm, and dinner from 7:30 pm to 9:30 pm. The weekly menu is posted on the mess notice board every Sunday evening.

## Special diets

Students who need a special diet for medical reasons can request it from the mess committee with a doctor's note. Jain food is available on request.

## Mess rebate

Students away from the hostel for 5 or more consecutive days can claim a mess rebate. The rebate request must be submitted on the hostel portal before leaving.

## Campus canteen

The campus canteen near the Aspen Block is open from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm on all working days. It accepts cash and UPI payments.
